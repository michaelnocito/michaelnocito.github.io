About

Why these are written this way

Most growing advice tells you what to do and skips the part where you decide whether it is time. That is the only hard part. A head that looks finished from the front can be three weeks out.

So every guide here leads with the signal. What you are looking at, what it looks like when it is ready, and what it looks like when it is not. Then the steps.

Photos are of my own plants, taken while doing the job. Where a guide gives a number, it comes from a university extension service or a seed-saving organization, and the source is linked at the bottom of the page.
